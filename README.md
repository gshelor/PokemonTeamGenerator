I got bored of picking the same team in pokemon every time I replay FireRed so when I found the Rokemon R package, I thought of how I could use it to randomly generate a pokemon team and potentially add a new form of intrigue/challenge to playing through the game. By randomly changing the seed based on what year and day it is, it will always generate a new team for me to play with in the game (or any others, should I adapt this method to other games).
